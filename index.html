Pathway to Venture

The 5-Stage Founder Journey

A structured institutional milestone roadmap guiding student and faculty innovators from the first raw flash of inspiration to an incorporated, funded company.

View Full Journey Map 🗺️
01 Intake
💡
Idea
Frame your problem statement, evaluate market novelty, and submit through the FIRE intake portal for formal institutional assessment.
Key Deliverable: Problem Dossier & Triage Route
02 Discovery
🧪
Validate
Conduct customer interviews, define your value proposition, verify product-market fit, and review feasibility with domain mentors.
Key Deliverable: Customer Discovery Evidence (30+ Interviews)
03 Build
🛠️
Prototype
Access the SCIEnT prototyping lab, 3D printing, PCB mills, and HPC clusters to engineer functional proofs-of-concept and MVPs.
Key Deliverable: Working Hardware or Software MVP
04 Test
🚀
Pilot
Deploy beta trials across the 800-acre NITT campus or with industry challenge sponsors. Unlock proof-of-concept seed grants.
Key Deliverable: Field Trial Metrics & Seed Grant (₹10L)
05 Incorporate
🏢
Venture
Formal private limited incorporation, patent protection through NITT IPR Cell, investor pitch days, and external seed capital.
Key Deliverable: Incorporated Entity & Seed Round
