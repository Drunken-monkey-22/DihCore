Access Workflow

How Facility Access Works

A standardized 5-step operational pipeline ensuring safe equipment utilization, verified training, and priority project allocation.

1

Identify & Check Specs

Search the facility directory for required machines, tool tolerances, consumable materials, and eligibility guidelines.

Deliverable: Tool list & specs
2

Submit Access Form

Click 'Start Access Request' on the facility card. Specify your project title, faculty supervisor, and requested run hours.

Turnaround: 2–5 working days
3

Safety & Competency

Complete the mandatory lab safety induction. Demonstrate equipment operating knowledge with the laboratory technician.

Output: Lab clearance badge
4

Slot Booking & Access

Receive slot confirmation via email. Smartcard/biometric entry credentials are programmed for the approved duration.

Status: Biometric access live
5

Session Log & Post-Work

Operate machinery according to safety SOPs. Log machine runtime hours in the digital register and clear waste materials.

Completion: Usage log signed
🦺

Mandatory Laboratory Safety Rules

All laboratory attendees must wear certified Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) including safety glasses, lab coats, and closed-toe footwear where designated. Equipment must never be operated unattended or overnight without explicit written permission from the Laboratory In-Charge.
