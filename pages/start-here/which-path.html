Individual Student

B.Tech / B.Arch Undergraduates
Early Concept
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Registered undergraduate student at NIT Trichy in good academic standing.
  • • A clearly articulated problem statement (even without code or physical hardware).
  • • Willingness to attend an initial 15-minute diagnostic call or Idea Clinic session.
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Submit safe 5-step intake form via FIRE without disclosing secret IP.
  • 2. Receive assigned domain coordinator review and triage within 3–5 days.
  • 3. Attend weekend Idea Clinic for customer validation and pitch refinement.
  • 4. Obtain SCIEnT prototyping badge credentials and mentor matching.
Responsible Office FIRE Operations & SCIEnT

Idea Clinics Alumni Mentors SCIEnT 3D Printing Director's Seed Fund Teammate Matching

Club Team

SPIDER, EFlames, AARUUSH, E-Cell & Technical Teams
Team Initiative
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Official recognition as an active NITT technical club or student competition team.
  • • Endorsement from designated faculty advisor or technical club coordinator.
  • • Defined project scope with verifiable team leads and deliverables.
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Submit Club Project Listing via authorized Club Admin portal.
  • 2. Faculty coordinator reviews and endorses listing within 3 days.
  • 3. Project published in public directory with recruitment callouts.
  • 4. Reserve dedicated SCIEnT workbench spaces and annual showcase slots.

Faculty Research

Translational Lab Research & Faculty Spin-Offs
R&C Aligned
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Full-time NITT faculty member acting as Principal Investigator (PI).
  • • Ongoing or completed sponsored research project with commercial translation potential.
  • • Compliance with NITT R&C and Institutional IP Policy guidelines.
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Register translational project on FIRE Faculty Research Portal.
  • 2. Post student research assistant opportunities for UG/PG students.
  • 3. Liaise with NITT IPR Cell for patentability analysis and provisional filing.
  • 4. Apply for translational funding grants (NIDHI Prayas, MeitY TIDE 2.0).
Responsible Office Dean (Research & Consultancy) & IPR Cell
🛠️ Available Support Types
Student Fellow Recruitment Patent Drafting & Filing TIDE 2.0 Grants Industry Licensing Spin-off Entity Setup

PG Research Project

M.Tech, M.Sc, MS by Research & Ph.D Scholars
Deep Tech PoC
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Registered post-graduate or doctoral student at NIT Trichy.
  • • Thesis research exhibiting Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 3 or above.
  • • Faculty guide concurrence for commercialisation exploration.
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Complete FIRE idea submission specifying PG/Ph.D thesis context.
  • 2. Schedule GPU HPC cluster scheduling or Nanotech clean room hours.
  • 3. Conduct prior-art check through IPR Cell before thesis defense publication.
  • 4. Pitch to deep-tech seed committees for NIDHI EIR or Prayas grants.
Responsible Office Dean (Academic) & Dean (R&C)

GPU HPC Credits Nanotech Clean Room Prior-Art Searches NIDHI EIR Fellowship Thesis Spin-off Advisory

Post-SIH Team

Smart India Hackathon Continuation Track
Dedicated Track
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Participation in Smart India Hackathon (Hardware or Software edition).
  • • Minimum 50% NITT student composition in the core continuing team.
  • • Functional codebase or prototype demonstrating original hackathon output.
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Complete Post-SIH continuation application with ministry PS code.
  • 2. Verification of competition IP rules and ministry follow-up terms.
  • 3. Onboard to 6-week structured milestone sprint with lab access.
  • 4. Present at Demo Day for formal pre-incubation and seed grant review.
Responsible Office FIRE SIH Coordination Cell

Incorporated Startup

Registered Private Limited / LLP / OPC
Venture Stage
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • Legally incorporated entity (MCA CIN / LLPIN active).
  • • At least one founder must be a current NITT student, faculty, or verified alumnus.
  • • DPIIT Startup Recognition certificate (or in process).
📋 Sequential Process
  • 1. Register company profile in the official NITT Startup Directory.
  • 2. Apply for on-campus incubation desks and laboratory testing space.
  • 3. Submit application for Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS).
  • 4. Pitch to quarterly Incubation Evaluation Committee for grant approval.
Responsible Office NITT Institutional Incubation Board
🛠️ Available Support Types
SISFS Grant (up to ₹20L) SISFS Debt (up to ₹50L) Campus Co-working Desks Internship Hiring Investor Demo Days

Alumni / External Founder

Alumni Mentors, Corporates & Ecosystem Partners
Partner Track
🎯 Entry Criteria
  • • NIT Trichy alumnus OR external corporate organisation / angel investor.
