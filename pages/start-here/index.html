Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Get immediate clarity on how FIRE works, eligibility requirements, intellectual property safeguards, and next steps.

What is FIRE and how does it differ from a student club?
+

FIRE (Foundation for Innovation & Research Entrepreneurship) is NIT Trichy’s official institutional coordination platform. Unlike a student club that focuses on extra-curricular workshops or hackathons, FIRE acts as the central administrative and physical conduit uniting academic departments, prototyping facilities (SCIEnT, HPC, Nanotech), the NITT IPR Cell, alumni venture networks, and government funding bodies under one roof.

Who is eligible to submit an idea or request facility access?
+

All bona fide NIT Trichy undergraduate students, post-graduate scholars, doctoral researchers, faculty members, and verified alumni are eligible to submit ideas, register research projects, or apply for prototyping facilities. External founders can engage through industry challenge submissions, mentor registrations, or joint corporate R&D partnerships.

Does submitting an idea to FIRE compromise my intellectual property?
+

No. Submitting an intake form does not grant NIT Trichy or any reviewer ownership of your intellectual property. Our intake form explicitly advises you not to disclose unreleased proprietary trade secrets or sensitive algorithms. Once your submission is processed, the NITT IPR Cell provides confidential guidance on filing provisional patents before any public showcase or external investor pitching.

What kind of financial assistance or grants does FIRE provide?
+

FIRE coordinates access to multiple non-dilutive grant mechanisms. For early-stage validation, the NITT Director's Discretionary Fund provides micro-grants up to ₹2 Lakhs. For prototype development, students and faculty can be routed into DST-NIDHI Prayas (up to ₹10 Lakhs). Incorporated ventures can be considered for Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) grants and convertible debt up to ₹50 Lakhs.

How long does the review process take once I submit my idea?
+

Initial intake screening takes between 3 to 5 working days. You will receive an automated Request ID immediately upon submission. Within 5 working days, a domain coordinator will contact you via your NITT institutional email to schedule an initial 15-minute diagnostic call or invite your team to an upcoming Idea Clinic session.
