The 9 Venture Development Stages

Click a stage to update details
01
Idea Discovery Phase: Intake
Workshops · Hackathons · Faculty Projects
Ideation through hackathons, domain workshops, faculty lab research, and published corporate challenges.
02
Initial Assessment Phase: Triage
Intake Screening · Needs Identification
Structured 5-step intake review by FIRE operations to identify technical domain, IP risks, and required facilities.
03
Validation Phase: Research
User Research · Customer Interviews · Mentor Sync
Rigorous hypothesis testing, stakeholder interviews, and 1:1 mentor advisory to establish problem-market relevance.
04
Team Formation Phase: Team
Talent Matching · Role Delegation · Advisor Onboarding
Assembling complementary skill sets across hardware, software, design, and domain expertise.
05
Prototyping & PoC Phase: Build
SCIEnT Labs · Clean Room · HPC Clusters
Hands-on engineering, 3D fabrication, electronics assembly, and initial bench testing of working hardware or software.
06
IP Assessment Phase: Legal
NITT IPR Cell · Prior Art · Provisional Filing
Formal referral to the NITT IPR Cell for patentability assessment, freedom-to-operate checks, and non-disclosure strategy.
07
Pilot Deployment Phase: Test
Campus Test Beds · Industry Partners · Municipalities
Deploying prototypes in realistic operating environments (campus water systems, factories, partner hospitals).
08
Incorporation Phase: Venture
Pvt Ltd Formation · Legal Advisory · Bank Onboarding
Formal legal company registration, founding equity split agreements, and compliance setups with professional guidance.
09
Follow-On Support Phase: Growth
Seed Grants · Investor Pitch Days · Scale Strategy
Unlocking government grant pipelines (NIDHI, SISFS, TIDE 2.0), corporate pilots, and alumni angel syndicates.
● Stage 01 of 09 · Discovery Phase

Idea Discovery

The initial spark where problems are observed, defined, and structured into workable technical hypotheses.

🔍 What Happens in this Stage
Innovators discover problems through coursework, faculty lab research, hackathons like SIH, or real-world corporate challenges published on FIRE. Teams attend ideation clinics to transform vague concepts into actionable problem statements.
👥 Who Helps & Key Partners
Academic faculty supervisors, student technical clubs (SPIDER, E-Flames), and industry challenge sponsors.
