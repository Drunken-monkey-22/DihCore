FIRE-2024 Prototype Under Review

IoT Water Quality Analyzer for Campus Tanks

ℹ️
Evaluation in Progress: Your submission was reviewed by the initial FIRE screening panel on Oct 14. Currently assigned to Dr. Rajesh Kumar (Civil Engg) for lab feasibility review. Expected remarks by Nov 02, 2026.
Problem Statement

Hostels and academic complexes at NIT Trichy store treated water in over 45 overhead tanks. Routine physical sampling happens bi-weekly, meaning contamination or filtration failures often go undetected for multiple days.

Proposed Solution & Technical Architecture

A solar-backed, non-invasive probe unit installed at tank access ports. Measures pH (±0.1), Turbidity (0-1000 NTU), and TDS in real time. Sends encrypted telemetry over NB-IoT to a campus dashboard; triggers automated SMS alerts to Estate Maintenance if WHO drinking water parameters are exceeded.

Target Users
NITT Estate Office, Student Residents, Municipal Water Authorities
Lead Applicant & Team
Aravind Selvam (CSE), Karthick Raja (ECE)
Requested Facilities
SCIEnT Prototyping Lab (PCB Bench, 3D Printer), Environmental Engg Wet Lab
Recommended Funding Track
NIDHI Prayas PoC Grant / NITT Director's Fund
