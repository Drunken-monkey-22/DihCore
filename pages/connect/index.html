How Connections Work on FIRE

Every connection is coordinated transparently through verified NITT channels, ensuring reliable follow-through, prompt responses, and institutional accountability.

📝
01

Submit Your Request

State your objectives clearly — whether booking a mentor slot, recruiting specific skills for your project, or submitting a corporate problem statement with desired deliverables.

⏱️ Takes ~3 minutes • Instant ID issued
🔍
02

Review & Match

The FIRE operations team and departmental coordinators evaluate requirements for alignment, verify institutional affiliations, and route the proposal to optimal advisors or candidates.

⚡ SLA: 3–5 working days • Peer moderation
🤝
03

Connect & Build

Receive warm mutual introductions with calendar invites, secure collaboration guidelines, and continued milestone tracking through the My FIRE founder dashboard.

Privacy & Intellectual Property Assurance

All discussions, project briefs, challenge submissions, and mentorship exchanges facilitated through FIRE are strictly governed by NIT Trichy's institutional ethics and IP framework. Confidential challenges and proprietary deep-tech disclosures are safeguarded by mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before access is granted. Student and faculty inventors retain full recognized ownership in accordance with institute policies.
